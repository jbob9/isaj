## Design Context

### Users
Parents with children aged 2–18 in the Croix-des-Bouquets / Bon-Repos area of Haiti, primarily French-speaking. They are researching schools, comparing options for their children, and deciding whether to enroll. Secondary users: prospective teachers, community members. Context: viewed on mobile (often lower-end Android) and desktop; connection quality varies. The audience has moderate digital literacy — they need clear, trustworthy information, not novelty.

### Brand Personality
**Academically rigorous · Warm and community-rooted · Aspirational**

ISAJ was founded in 2011 by two young Haitians with limited means but deep conviction. The brand should feel earned, not flashy. Premium without being cold. Educational without being institutional. A school that punches above its weight.

### Aesthetic Direction
Editorial school magazine meets premium Haitian institution. Think a well-produced academic prospectus: dense but airy, organized but warm.

- **Theme**: Light (school environment, parents browsing during the day, paper-like feel)
- **Color palette**: Cool near-black (ink #0b1220) as dominant surface, single blue accent (brand #2657ee, deep #1b3fb5), warm paper background (#f4f6fb). Every neutral is subtly tinted toward the brand blue.
- **Typography**: Plus Jakarta Sans (body/UI — warm, humanist, highly legible) + Lora (display serif accents — scholarly, on-screen readable). These replace the earlier Fraunces which was too stylistically quirky.
- **References**: A high-end French lycée website, a premium Haitian cultural institution, the admissions page of a serious private school.
- **Anti-references**: Nothing that looks like a generic WordPress school theme. No stock photo smiles, no banner sliders, no clipart graduation caps.

### Design Principles
1. **Legibility over decoration** — Every type choice, spacing decision, and color must make information easier to parse, not harder. This audience is decision-making, not browsing for entertainment.
2. **Earned premium** — Visual richness through restraint: tight typographic control, well-used whitespace, a limited palette executed with precision. Not flashy.
3. **Mobile-first reality** — Haiti has high mobile penetration. Every layout must work beautifully at 390px before 1280px. Touch targets 44px minimum, no hover-only states.
4. **Authentic voice** — Real school data only (founded 2011, Osnel SAINT JUSTE, address: 5 Impasse Bernard Bon-Repos). No fictional placeholder people or generic testimonials.
5. **French language throughout** — Every label, CTA, error state, and micro-copy in correct literary French, no anglicisms.
